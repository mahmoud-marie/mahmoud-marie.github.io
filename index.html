About

I'm a Media Buyer with 2 years of experience running paid campaigns on Meta, Google, TikTok, Snapchat, and ChatGPT Ads. I have worked with more than 200 clients, mostly in e-commerce and the Gulf market.

I handle the full process — campaign strategy, targeting, creative testing, tracking, and budget optimization. I use tools like GA4, GTM, Pixels, and UTM tracking to understand what happens after someone clicks the ad, not just the click itself.

For me, media buying is not only about launching campaigns. It's about testing, reading the data, finding what is stopping better results, and making smarter decisions with the budget.
